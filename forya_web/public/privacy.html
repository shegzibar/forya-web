GamingHub Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Overview

GamingHub ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

By using GamingHub, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. What Information We Collect

Account & Profile

  • Phone number or email address
  • Full name
  • Password
  • Profile photo (optional)

Gaming & Bookings

  • Booking dates, times, and duration
  • Gaming center location selected
  • Room type and pricing
  • Session history

Wallet & Payments

  • Wallet balance and transaction history
  • Payment methods used (Cash, InstaPay, Vodafone Cash, Fawry)
  • Payment records

Reviews & Ratings

  • Ratings and comments you leave on gaming centers

Photos (Cyber Owners)

  • Cover photos of your gaming center
  • Interior and room photos
  • Gallery images

Device & Usage

  • Device type and OS version
  • IP address
  • Device ID
  • How you use the app
  • Pages you visit
  • Time spent in the app

Location

  • GPS coordinates (if you enable it)
  • Your city/area
  • Gaming centers you search for

3. How We Use Your Information

To Provide Service

  • Process bookings and reservations
  • Send booking confirmations and reminders
  • Manage your wallet and points system
  • Respond to support requests

To Improve

  • Understand how users interact with the app
  • Fix bugs and improve performance
  • Develop new features
  • Analyze usage patterns

To Communicate

  • Send push notifications about bookings
  • Send emails about service updates
  • Send promotional messages (you can opt-out)

To Protect

  • Detect and prevent fraud
  • Prevent illegal activity
  • Protect user safety
  • Comply with laws

To Personalize

  • Show relevant gaming centers near you
  • Customize your experience
  • Recommend features

4. Who We Share Your Information With

Gaming Centers

When you book, the gaming center receives:

  • Your name
  • Booking details (date, time, room, price)
  • Your wallet ID (for top-up transactions)
  • Any reviews or ratings you leave

Our Service Partners

We use third-party services to run GamingHub. They may access your data as needed to provide their services, but they cannot use it for other purposes.

Legal Requirements

We may share information if required by law or to:

  • Protect our rights
  • Protect user safety
  • Prevent illegal activity
  • Comply with court orders

Business Transfer

If GamingHub is acquired or merged, your data may be transferred as part of the business transaction.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encrypted data transmission
  • Secure password encryption
  • Regular security checks
  • Access controls
  • Secure authentication

However, no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute protection.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Account Information: Kept while you have an account. Deleted when you delete your account (with some exceptions for legal requirements).
  • Booking History: Kept for 2 years for billing and dispute purposes.
  • Wallet & Payments: Kept for 3 years for financial records.
  • Support Messages: Kept for 1 year after resolution.
  • Photos: Stored until you delete them.

7. Your Rights

You Can:

  • Access all your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account
  • Download your data
  • Opt-out of promotional emails
  • Disable location services in your device settings

How to Submit Requests:

Email us at: privacy@gaminghub.app

Include: your name, your account email, and the type of request (access, delete, correct, etc.).

We'll respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

GamingHub is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If we discover we've collected data from a child under 18, we will delete it immediately.

If you're a parent/guardian concerned about your child's data, contact us at privacy@gaminghub.app

9. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you logged in
  • Remember your preferences
  • Analyze app usage
  • Improve performance

You can disable cookies in your device settings, but some features may not work properly.

10. Third-Party Links

GamingHub may include links to external websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Read their privacy policies before sharing information.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. We'll notify you by:

  • Posting updates in the app
  • Sending you an email
  • Asking you to accept the new policy

Continued use of GamingHub means you accept the updated policy.

12. Your Choices

  • Location Sharing: You can disable location access in your device's settings at any time.
  • Notifications: You can turn off push notifications in app settings.
  • Promotional Emails: You can opt-out by clicking "Unsubscribe" in any email.
  • Cookies: You can disable cookies in your browser/device settings.

13. Data Transfers

Your information may be stored and processed in different locations for backup, redundancy, and service improvement purposes.

14. Contact Us

Privacy Questions or Requests:

Email: privacy@gaminghub.app

Response time: 30 days
