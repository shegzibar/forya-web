Why Forya

Built around how you actually play.

Forya started with a familiar frustration: showing up to your usual gaming center after class or work, only to find every seat taken. So we asked centers to publish their seats in real time — the same way you'd check if a table's free before driving across town.

Now, whether you're squeezing in a quick match solo or organizing a weekend tournament with friends, the booking part stays out of the way. Open the app, see what's actually open right now, and reserve it before someone else does.
